*Ongoing · 2 days* — Greg Brockman and Dan Roberts announced three AI-produced results, and Ryan Williams and David Gross are leading independent reviews. A day later roon used the announcement to argue humans will keep being surprised by AI and then lose control.

- Tue: OpenAI announced Lean-verified results on local smoothing and the Kakeya conjecture, a refutation of ETH, and existence of QCD and completions of the Standard Model; Williams, Impagliazzo and Paturi were asked to examine the ETH claim.
- Tue: Tenobrus called it 'completely fucking insane,' while Roko said the barely subexponential algorithm was 'somewhat deflationary' and posters noted the physics proofs are not yet public.
- Wed: roon, quoting Roberts, wrote that fighting a runaway intelligence explosion looks like 'wow I didn't know you could DO that with mere bits and atoms' and then losing; davidad said losing human dominance may be on the path to winning.
- Wed: Axiom separately announced a full Lean formalization of the 15,000-page Classification of Finite Simple Groups.

**How these connect:** roon's Wednesday post directly quoted Dan Roberts' announcement of OpenAI's discoveries from Tuesday.

## Reading single neurons in Qwen: a green neuron, a 34,220-neuron persona library, J-lens, one-neuron owls, and the Neuron Lens database

*Ongoing · 5 days* — Community researchers including Sauers, josh, a9lim, treetown and max! spent the week finding, steering and reading individual neurons in open models, while thebes warned that persona effects may not mean persona-specific neurons.

- Mon: Sauers posted 'I found a neuron that makes the model green,' and roon called monosemanticity 'a pathway to many abilities.'
- Wed: josh, inspired by Sauers, published a searchable library of 34,220 Qwen persona neurons rated for weirdness by Haiku; mixing 'yeast starter' and 'existential crisis' made 'a humble muffin.'
- Thu–Fri: a9lim previewed J-lens for reading what neurons represent; max! turned Qwen3-8B into an owl by training one neuron, and thebes argued steering causes 'persona solving,' which josh conceded was 'sloppy framing.'
- Sat: a9lim and treetown released Neuron Lens, a database of every neuron and attention head in Qwen 3 8B and Gemma 4 12B, whose R-lens predicts steering results over 90% of the time.

**How these connect:** All are the same community thread about interpreting individual neurons in Qwen-family models: josh credited Sauers for his library, thebes responded to the persona-neuron trend, and Neuron Lens is the released version of a9lim's J-lens.
