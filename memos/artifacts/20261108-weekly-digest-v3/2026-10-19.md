## PHP bugs leak before the patch, the NSA says 'take your system offline,' and the breaches keep coming

*Ongoing · 3 days* — On Wednesday two critical PHP flaws leaked before their fix, and governments told site owners to pull servers offline. GitLab, Railway, Linux kernel leaks and a Quest Diagnostics breach followed over the next two days. Posters argued over why so much dropped at once, crediting AI-assisted bug hunting, and by Saturday OpenAI and Cloudflare's CEO were saying this is the new normal. It was the week's dominant story by engagement and the one most community members joined.

## Anthropic puts ~20% of its shares toward public benefit; Musk answers by pledging a SpaceX share to every American kid

*Ongoing · 2 days* — Anthropic announced a Public Benefit Fund that starts with 5% of its shares and can grow with the company's valuation. @elonmusk replied that shares should go directly to the public. The next day he pledged one SpaceX share to every kid in America, the week's single biggest post. The community argued over whether children, Trump Accounts or other groups should be the target of this new wave of redistribution.

Anthropic's announcement drew Musk's 'give the shares directly to the public' reply and teasing about EAs.

> @AnthropicAI: We’re committing a portion of Anthropic’s shares to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a new wholly owned entity dedicated entirely to the public benefit. It will start off with 5% of our shares. For every 50% increase in the company’s value over a set baseline, we can contribute up to an extra 2%, subject to our board’s discretion. In total, this means that roughly 20% of Anthropic’s shares are committed to the public good. Our co-founders have already pledged to donate 80% of their equity, and around half our employees are donating a portion of their equity as part of a donation matching program we’ve run since our founding. In the post below, we share more details about our new fund. https://t.co/XzDntVH0vH

Musk followed through with a pledge that passed half a million likes.

> @elonmusk: Next year, I will give one of my SpaceX shares to every kid in America. This will be the first of many.
